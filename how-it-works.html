    How it's built

    retake-vrs rewrites Valve's ranking model in Python, checks it against every figure Valve publishes, and uses it to simulate the race to each Major.

    Which matches count, and when the Majors are decided

    Each row is one of Valve's monthly standings. Its bar covers the six months of matches it counts: the last month at full weight, older matches fading out. The pink columns are the Majors, linked to the standings that sent their invitations.

    Counted, losing weight with age Last month, full weight Major

    1 · The model

    Valve's ranking model, rewritten in Python from an understanding of it, with none of its code copied. It matches Valve's values within 4.6 × 10⁻¹³ on the reference sample.

    2 · The audit

    Every starting value, head-to-head adjustment and factor Valve publishes is recomputed, within its rounding.

    3 · Independent data

    OpenVRS's matches, from Liquipedia, rebuild Valve's standings: 30 of 30 in the top 30.

    4 · The race

    Every event up to the invitation date is played 5,000 times. Backtested on two Majors.
