    How the race is simulated

    Projection · retake-vrs

    The chances on this site do not come from the points alone. They come from simulated races, kept apart from the ranking: the points say where a team stands, the simulations what could happen next.

    What we predict

    Which teams Valve will invite to a Major. In each region, Valve invites the best ranked teams of the standings published on the invitation date, up to that region's number of spots.

    Which teams, which spots

    Every ranked team, in its region, against the spots Valve announced for the Major.

    For PGL Major Singapore 2026: Europe 18, Americas 9, Asia 5.

    What is simulated

    We start from the standings a month before the invitation date. Every event between that day and the invitation date is played again, with its real teams and its real format. Each match is won with the chance the ranking model gives from the two teams' points, with the gap toned down because the model is too sure of favourites. Prize money goes out again by simulated placement. Then Valve's model computes the standings on the invitation date, and the spots are handed out.

    From races to a chance

    The race is played 5,000 times. A team's chance is the share of those races in which it gets a spot. A team invited in 3,000 races out of 5,000 gets 60%.

    3,000 / 5,000 = 60%

    What a chance means

    60% is not a promise. Among all the teams given about 60%, about six in ten should be invited and four not. We check it on past Majors, a month before their invitations:

    • PGL Major Singapore 2026: 24 of the 32 invited teams had more than 50%, and 5 of the 7 teams given 60 to 80% were invited.
    • IEM Cologne Major 2026: 26 of the 32 invited teams had more than 50%, and 4 of the 7 teams given 60 to 80% were invited.

    Limits

    • So far, only past Majors: the race is played again with the events, teams and formats that really happened. Projecting the next Major needs data we do not have yet.
    • A team's strength is fixed for the month: a team on a run does not get stronger during the race.
    • Teams that came from far below are underrated: on both past Majors, most of the misses climbed from outside the spots.
    • Teams that qualified for an event during the month are known in advance.

    The full method, with every measurement, is in docs/race.md.
