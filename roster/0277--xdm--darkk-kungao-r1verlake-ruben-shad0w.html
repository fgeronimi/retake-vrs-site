    1. Four scores between 0 and 1

      Money of the teams beaten Bounty collectediThe bounties of the teams it beat, over its 10 best wins, weighted by age and by the event's prize pool. Beating a team that has won a lot is worth more. 1.0 means as much as the team ranked 5th, or more.0.272
      Teams beaten Opponent networkiOver its 10 best wins, how many different teams each beaten opponent had beaten itself, weighted by age and by the event's prize pool. Beating teams that beat many others is worth more. 1.0 means as much as the team ranked 5th, or more.0.010
      LAN winsiIts 10 best wins at LAN events, where both teams play in the same venue, older wins counting less. 1.0 means as many as the team ranked 5th, or more.0.000
