    How this score is built

    1. Four scores between 0 and 1

      Prize money won Bounty offerediThe prize money the team won in its 10 best results, older results counting less. Valve calls it the bounty a team offers: what beating it is worth. 1.0 means as much as the team ranked 5th, or more.0.393
      Money of the teams beaten Bounty collectediThe bounties of the teams it beat, over its 10 best wins, weighted by age and by the event's prize pool. Beating a team that has won a lot is worth more. 1.0 means as much as the team ranked 5th, or more.0.432
      Teams beaten Opponent networkiOver its 10 best wins, how many different teams each beaten opponent had beaten itself, weighted by age and by the event's prize pool. Beating teams that beat many others is worth more. 1.0 means as much as the team ranked 5th, or more.0.250
      LAN winsiIts 10 best wins at LAN events, where both teams play in the same venue, older wins counting less. 1.0 means as many as the team ranked 5th, or more.0.998

      Average of the four: 0.518

    2. The starting value

      Valve spreads the averages of all rosters over 400 to 2,000 points, from the lowest to the highest. An average of 0.518 gives BC.Game 1,371.1 points.

      Recomputed by retake-vrs: within Valve's rounding.

      Each match then adds or removes points: more for a surprise, less for an old match. 30 matches (20 won, 10 lost) moved BC.Game by −34.2.
