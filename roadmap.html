    Roadmap

    What the project wants to do, what is already done, and what is missing to do the rest. Most of what is left waits for data we do not have yet.

    Data we do not have yet

    Upcoming events, their teams and their schedule

    To project the race to an invite date that has not happened yet, the simulation needs the matches still to play. Today it only replays a month that is over.

    Blocks

    • Project the race to future invite dates, for every VRS event #74
    • A detail per team: place distribution, invite line, next event #76
    • Pick match results and recompute every chance in the browser #77

    The real format of each event (groups, brackets, best-of)

    The data does not record formats, so the simulation guesses them from the number of teams. Events with group stages are played as plain brackets, which changes how many matches each team plays.

    Blocks

    • Raise the backtest above 80% of invited teams given more than 50% #58
    • Project the race to future invite dates, for every VRS event #74

    Match results between two Valve standings

    Valve publishes its standings about once a month. Updating the chances every day needs the results of the days in between.

    Blocks

    • Update the chances every day, and show what moved since yesterday #75

    Past invite lists of every VRS event, with their rules

    We only have the rules of two Majors. Events from other organisers invite from the global ranking as well as by region, and checking our chances needs many past lists, not two.

    Blocks

    • Project the race to future invite dates, for every VRS event #74
    • Check the chances on every past invite list #78

    Teams that turned an invite down

    When a team declines, its spot passes down the ranking. Without past declines, any probability of declining would be made up.

    Blocks

    • Project the race to future invite dates, for every VRS event #74

    The date of each roster change

    A team that changes players during the month is simulated with its old lineup until the next standings.

    Blocks

    • Raise the backtest above 80% of invited teams given more than 50% #58
    • Follow roster changes during a race to a Major #60
